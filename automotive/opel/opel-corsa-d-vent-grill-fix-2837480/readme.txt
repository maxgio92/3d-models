OPEL Corsa vent grill fix by lorinczroby on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2837480

Summary:
A common issue of OPEL Corsa D is the broken vent grill. The horizontal adjustment not working, fins stuck.Looking into the issue I fount that there are mainly two root causes for this:1) The vertical fins are stuck, rotates very hard due to high friction (due to dirt, leak of lubricant etc.) with the vent case;2) The holder of the vertical fins knob is cracked as you can see in one of the attached pictures. This causes a lose of the vertical fins moving mechanism which loses mechanical contact to the vertical fins so they are not moving anymore.Here is what I have done to fix the problem:Cleaned the vent grill of dirt and old lubricant;Enlarged the holes into which the vertical fins are fixed by about 0.2 mm (top and bottom as well, see attached pictures) so they will rotate more easily;mounted a fixing mechanism for the fins rotating mechanism so mechanical contact is tighten, see attacked .stl file for the fixing piece.The fixing fiece is mounted using an M3 screw and not, you will have to drill a 3.5 mm hole in the case of the vent grill as shown in the pictures.lucricate mechanism and job done!
