Funnel Extension for the Wacaco Picopresso and 1ZPresso JMAX by DamianWarS on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5347930

Summary:
This is a funnel extension designed for the stock funnel to fit snuggly inside so it's easier to pour coffee into the basket and distribute it more aggressively without the risk of coffee spilling.  It also doubles as a funnel to load beans into a 1ZPresso JMAX.It's a generous size funnel and the stock funnel fits inside of it.  It's a snug fit so the two work as one rather than loosely fit over the top of it.  Alternatively, it also fits directly over the basket without the stock funnel so you can use it with or without the stock funnel.  using the stock funnel it's flush against the walls of the basket (as the stock funnel is designed for) using without the stock funnel it's still quite functional but doesn't cover the whole rim of the basket, you just might have to tap the basket to get the coffee to settle inside if coffee is along the rim.  The design still allows to tamp through the funnel as it sits over the stock funnel but the stock tamper has a wider top which is obstructed by the funnel to fully tamp through. You'd have to use a more traditional tamper that fits like from La Pavoni tamper.the JMAX by 1ZPresso has the same outer diameter as the Picopresso funnel so this funnel also fits perfectly with the JMAX which is handy if you have both.  I don't know about other 1ZPresso grinders but if it has the same outer diameter as the JMAX it should work.The design works well when you're using this in your kitchen or at a static location and is easier for workflow.  You can travel with it fine as well it just has to be packed separately from the Picopresso kit/JMAX (obviously).
